How to play

Free-for-all in a desert ghost town. Grab weapon crates, wreck everyone else, and be the last car running. Green crates repair you, purple ones refill your special.

Keyboard

W A S D or arrowsDrive and steer
SpaceHandbrake, for drifting round corners
J or ZMachine gun (hold)
K or XFire selected weapon
L or CSpecial weapon
Q ESwitch weapon
RFlip back onto the road if stuck
P or EscPause

Combo moves

Tap ↑ ↑ then fire for a weapon's first combo, or ↓ ↓ then fire for its second. On touch screens, swipe up or down on the Fire button. Combos cost 3 ammo.

Homing missilesRattler volley, Sky strike
MortarCarpet barrage, Bunker buster
MinesMine toss, Minefield
TorchRing of fire, Fireball

Touch and gamepad

Left thumb drives with the stick. Right side has Gun, Fire, Special, Swap and Drift. On a gamepad, triggers drive, X is the gun, A fires, Y is special, B drifts, bumpers switch weapons.

Tips

Missiles lose you behind buildings. Mortars arc over cover. Red barrels and gas pumps explode. Ramps and dunes launch you, and the water tower can come down on someone's head.
