    How to play

    Free-for-all in a desert ghost town. Grab weapon crates, wreck everyone else, and be the last car running. Green crates repair you, purple ones refill your special. You carry three heavy weapons at a time: a crate of a fourth throws out the one you have least of.

    Controls

    The machine gun fires by itself at any car in its sights, and holding a hard turn at speed drifts. Either can be switched off in Settings.

    Combos

    Every heavy weapon has an attack combo and a defensive combo. Each costs 3 ammo.

    Homing missilesRattler volley · Decoy flare (pulls missiles off you)
    Rocket podsRocket fan · Tail gunner (fires out the back)
    MortarCarpet barrage · Smoke screen (breaks lock-ons)
    MinesMine toss · Kickback (throws everyone off you)
    TorchFireball · Ring of fire

    Route 67

    A three-lap race through the canyon. At each fork you choose: the mine shaft (shorter, and homing missiles can't follow you in) or the canyon road; the gorge jump (fastest if you land it) or the switchback; the old highway or the slower sandy riverbed. Wreck and you're back on the track three seconds later, at the start of that stretch, with half your armour and no heavy weapons.

    Tips

    Homing missiles lose you behind buildings and in smoke. Rockets fly straight, so line them up. Mortars arc over cover. Red barrels and gas pumps explode. Ramps and dunes launch you, and the water tower can come down on someone's head.
